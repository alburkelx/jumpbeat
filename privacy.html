Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

The JumpBeat app does not collect, store or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking in the app.

Motion data

JumpBeat reads your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to detect and count jumps. This data is processed on your device in real time. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any device or usage identifiers.

Contact form (website only)

If you send us a message through the contact form on our support page, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to us through a form service (Formspree) so that we can reply. We use this information only to answer your message and do not share or sell it. This applies only to the website form, not to the app.

Third parties

The JumpBeat app does not include advertising or analytics services, and does not share any information with third parties. The only third-party service involved is the website contact form described above.

Children

JumpBeat does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact form on our support page.
