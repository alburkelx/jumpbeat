Getting a clean count

  • Hold your phone firmly against your chest, or put it in a snug pocket or armband.
  • A phone swinging loose in your hand adds extra bumps, so keep it steady.
  • After you tap Start you get a 3-second countdown to get set.

Common questions

It counted too many or too few jumps.
Change the sensitivity: Small hops for jump rope, Normal for most jumping, or Big jumps to ignore small hops.

Counting stopped.
Counting pauses if the phone locks or you switch to another app. The app keeps the screen awake while it counts.

I can't hear the jump sounds.
Jump sounds follow your ringer switch. Check that the phone is not on silent, that the volume in the app is up, and that the sound is not set to Off.

How is height calculated?
Height comes from your airtime, so treat it as an estimate. Running and hopping down stairs also leave the ground for a moment, so they can be counted too.

Do I need an account?
No. JumpBeat has no accounts, no subscription and no ads.
